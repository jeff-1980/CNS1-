We submit the manuscript "What does a vortex-generator retrofit cost the drivetrain? Fleet evidence for the load price of an energy gain at an operating wind farm" for consideration as a Research Paper in *Renewable Energy*.

Aerodynamic retrofits are now a common way to raise the output of operating wind farms, and their energy gain is routinely validated from SCADA data. The extra load that the gain passes to the drivetrain, the subsystem that dominates downtime in reliability surveys, has so far been studied only in simulation or on research turbines. Owners deciding whether to retrofit ageing turbines therefore have a measured energy benefit but no measured load cost.

Using the open SCADA data of the Hill of Towie wind farm, where one turbine was retrofitted with vortex generators nine months before the other 20, we measure both sides of this trade-off on commercial turbines. The retrofit raised energy by 4.8%, consistent with the operator's independent validation, and raised a quasi-static main-shaft torque damage-equivalent load by 2.3%. We introduce a load cost ratio, the relative load change per relative energy change, and find it to be 0.48 for an S-N exponent of 3, about 60% of what a simple power scaling implies. We show that this follows from the variable-speed control law and from the gain occurring at part load. The estimator is corrected for drift with 20 placebo turbines, checked against a known injected effect, and replicated on the later rollout to the remaining 20 turbines. The tests were fixed and hashed before they were run, and all deviations are reported.

We believe the work fits the journal's scope on wind energy conversion and the operation of renewable power plants. It gives operators a method, requiring only standard SCADA data, to price an upgrade in drivetrain load as well as in energy.

The manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under consideration elsewhere. Both authors approved the submission and declare no competing interests. The data are openly available, and all code is publicly released. Generative AI use is declared in the manuscript.
