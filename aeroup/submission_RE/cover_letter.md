Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Energy gain and drivetrain torque exposure of a vortex-generator retrofit: a field assessment at an operating wind farm" for consideration as a Research Paper in *Renewable Energy*.

Aerodynamic retrofits are now a common way to raise the output of operating wind farms, and their energy gain is routinely validated from SCADA data. The extra load that the gain passes to the drivetrain, the subsystem that dominates downtime in reliability surveys, has so far been studied only in simulation or on research turbines. Owners deciding whether to retrofit ageing turbines therefore have a measured energy benefit but no measured load cost.

Using the open SCADA data of the Hill of Towie wind farm, where one turbine was retrofitted with vortex generators nine months before the other 20, we measure both sides of this trade-off on commercial turbines. The retrofit raised energy by 4.8%, consistent with the operator's independent validation, and time at rated power by 1.7 percentage points. A quasi-static main-shaft torque exposure index rose by 0.48% per 1% of extra energy, below what pure power scaling implies, because the gain arose mainly at part load and the variable-speed controller passed part of it into rotor speed. We are explicit about what a single retrofitted turbine can show: the estimates are corrected for the drift of 20 placebo turbines, their uncertainty comes from a joint farm-wide bootstrap, and their strength is bounded with pseudo-retrofit dates, injected known effects and a screen for concurrent changes. The index measures load level, not fatigue damage, and we do not translate it into lifetime. The tests were fixed and hashed before they were run, and all deviations are reported.

We believe the work fits the journal's scope on wind energy conversion and the operation of renewable power plants. It gives operators a method, requiring only standard SCADA data, to screen the drivetrain load-level side of an upgrade alongside its energy gain.

The manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under consideration elsewhere. I declare no competing interests. The data are openly available, and all code is publicly released. Generative AI use is declared in the manuscript.
