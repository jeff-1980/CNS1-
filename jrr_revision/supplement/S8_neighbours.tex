\section*{S8. Comparison with the nearest prior work}
\noindent The search behind this table combined Consensus (three queries, October 2026: interval RUL evaluated through a maintenance rule; RUL uncertainty under distribution shift or sensor faults; decision-oriented evaluation of probabilistic prognostics) with the reference lists of the retrieved papers. Papers marked F were read in full text (author or repository version); papers marked A were assessed from the abstract and, where available, the publisher's summary. Columns: \emph{Interval} -- a prediction interval or RUL distribution is evaluated; \emph{Sensor shift} -- structured sensor perturbation (drift, bias, gain, failure) is applied at test time; \emph{Rule} -- the interval or distribution feeds an explicit maintenance rule; \emph{Both errors} -- premature and late maintenance outcomes are reported separately; \emph{Engine CI} -- uncertainty is resampled at the unit level.
